HOW TO PLAY

Guess the secret 5-letter word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a real word. After each guess the tiles change color:

  • Daily: everyone gets the same word each day. A new one unlocks at midnight.
  • Unlimited: play as many random words as you want.
  • Hard mode (in ⚙️): letters you've found must be used in later guesses.
