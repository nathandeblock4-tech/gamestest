Customize your cube

How to play: Space, ↑, W, click or tap to jump. Hold it down to keep jumping. 6 levels from Easy to Insane, and each hides 3 secret coins 🟡 (they only count when you finish the level in normal mode).
Yellow rings jump you in mid-air, blue rings flip your gravity. Yellow pads launch you high, pink pads give a small hop. Portals: pink = ship (hold to fly up), orange = ball (tap to flip gravity), green = back to cube, yellow / blue = upside-down / normal gravity, and arrow portals change your speed. Practice mode saves checkpoints automatically (Z places one, X removes the last). P / Esc pauses, R restarts, M mutes, F goes full screen.
